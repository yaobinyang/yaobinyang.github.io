2027

  1. ASCE 2027
    Fiber-optic Miniature Pore Pressure Probe: Design, Fabrication and Application
    Yaobin Yang*, Linqing Luo, Kenichi Soga, and Xiang Sun*
    In ASCE Conference 2027 (Accepted Abstract, Paper Forthcoming, ID: ASCE2027-288), 2027

2026

  1. GRL
    Transforming Critical Water Pipelines into Seismic Fault Movement Sensors
    Maksymilian Jasiak, Yaobin Yang*, Chien-Chih Wang, Shih-Hung Chiu, Linqing Luo, Peter Hubbard, Yuankun Xu, Youqi Zhang, Roland Burgmann, and Kenichi Soga
    Geophysical Research Letters, Vol. 53, Issue 22, 2026
    DOI HTML
    Demonstrates full-scale distributed fiber-optic strain and temperature sensing paired with Cosserat rod kinematic modeling on an operating municipal water transmission pipeline crossing the active Hayward Fault, resolving slow fault-driven ground deformation at millimeter-per-year precision.
  2. JOES Accepted
    Flow-Induced Vibration Suppression for Distributed Fiber Optic Sensing Cables in Turbulent Marine Environments
    Linqing Luo*, Yaobin Yang, Kenichi Soga, John Ryan, and Yuxin Wu
    Journal of Ocean Engineering and Science, 2026 (Accepted)
    Investigates hydrodynamic vibration suppression strategies for marine distributed fiber-optic sensing cables subjected to turbulent ocean currents, improving acoustic and strain signal fidelity for offshore infrastructure and seafloor monitoring.
  3. ASCE JGGE
    Distributed Fiber Optic Sensing for Monitoring Piles under Eccentric Load
    Gyu-Beom Shin, Yaobin Yang*, Loizos Pelecanos, Federica Angela Mevoli, Andrew Yeskoo, Tom Shantz, and Kenichi Soga
    Journal of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering, Vol. 152, Issue 9, 04026089, 2026
    DOI HTML
    Field investigation of cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) piles subjected to eccentric loading, combining continuous distributed fiber-optic sensing (DFOS), 3D Cosserat rod shape reconstruction with stochastic error envelopes, and coupled axial-lateral finite element inverse analysis to quantify nonlinear soil reaction curves and construction-induced eccentricities.
  4. CompGeo
    Inference of pile capacity from distributed strain sensing via PDE-constrained optimization
    Yaobin Yang, Gyu-Beom Shin*, Loizos Pelecanos, Chien-Chih Wang, Linqing Luo, and Kenichi Soga
    Computers and Geotechnics, Vol. 196, 108131, 2026
    DOI
    Formulates a partial-differential-equation-constrained optimization (PDECO) inverse modeling framework that assimilates high-resolution distributed fiber-optic strain profiles to infer shaft friction mobilization, base resistance, and nonlinear soil-pile interaction directly from continuum equilibrium constraints.
  5. SSRN Under Review
    A Matrix-Free Discontinuous Galerkin Method for Dynamic Cosserat Rod Simulation
    Yaobin Yang*, Teng Zhang, Krishna Kumar, Kenichi Soga, Claudio Matias Perez, Linqing Luo, and Gyu-Beom Shin
    Under Review at Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering (SSRN Preprint 7417101), 2026
    SSRN DOI
    Develops a high-order matrix-free Discontinuous Galerkin formulation for nonlinear dynamic Cosserat rods on SE(3), enabling efficient simulation of large deformations, instabilities, and frictional contact in slender structures, bioinspired self-burrowing systems, and marine cables.
